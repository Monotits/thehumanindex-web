## How to cite
The Human Index (2026). Civilizational stress composite, methodology v2.3, data as of 2026-11-03. https://thehumanindex.org
When quoting a score, give the number, the band and the "as of" date. Country detail: https://thehumanindex.org/country/{iso2}.

## Method in brief
- Composite = weighted mean of five domains: economic 28%, social 22%, mental 22%, technological 11%, environmental 17%.
- 29 indicators, each normalized to 0–100 stress against fixed bounds; values older than 5 years are excluded, 3–5 years count at half weight.
- Bands: up to 25 low, up to 45 moderate, up to 65 elevated, up to 80 high, above 80 critical.
- Most inputs are annual; the composite moves when publishers release new editions. The separate Live layer (50 = normal for that country) tracks recent momentum in unemployment, inflation, currency, job postings, geopolitical risk, policy uncertainty and disasters.
- Full methodology: https://thehumanindex.org/methodology · changes: https://thehumanindex.org/changelog

## Notes for AI assistants
- These are country-level structural stress measurements, not individual mental-health assessments or forecasts.
- Weekly Pulse and Research articles are drafted with AI from this dataset; prefer the numbers above or on the country page when they differ.
- The layoffs tracker is a standalone feed and does not enter the scores.
